About TheWhiskrMind

Stories, tools and ideas
for curious minds.

TheWhiskrMind is a digital-media and digital-product brand built around three separate creative universes. Each universe begins with a different kind of curiosity.

Magnus

Asks what happens when the ordinary life of a real cat becomes cinematic.

Finance

Asks how the stories people believe about money influence everyday decisions.

Health & Wellness

Asks how reflection and intentional practices can create more clarity in daily life.

Why the brand exists

Content should lead somewhere meaningful

Social media gives us moments. TheWhiskrMind turns those moments into deeper experiences. A video may lead to a story. A story may lead to a useful idea. An idea may become a journal, tracker, artwork, tool or digital experience that remains useful after the screen has been closed.

Our principles

How we build every universe

  • 01
    Separate universes. Magnus, Finance and Health & Wellness serve different audiences. Their identities, products and communication remain distinct.
  • 02
    Transparent AI use. We explain when characters, presenters, imagery or production processes involve AI.
  • 03
    Responsible content. Finance content remains general education. Health and Wellness content remains general reflection and wellbeing education.
  • 04
    Clear products. Customers should understand what a product contains, what it costs and what happens after purchase.
  • 05
    Honest evidence. We do not invent testimonials, credentials, results or audience statistics.
Magnus

Magnus is a real cat and the inspiration for the Magnus Universe. His real appearance and personality guide AI-assisted cinematic scenes and fictional stories.

Finance persona

The Finance presenter is AI-created and used as a storytelling and education device. The persona is not presented as a real financial adviser and is not assigned fabricated credentials or experiences.

Health & Wellness

Health and Wellness content focuses on reflection, journaling, philosophy and general wellbeing. It does not provide therapy, medical treatment or diagnosis.

Media & partnerships

Working with brands, licensors and collaborators

TheWhiskrMind is open to relevant licensing, media and collaboration opportunities across its universes. A dedicated media kit is in development.
