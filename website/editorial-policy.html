Standards

Editorial Policy

TheWhiskrMind publishes entertainment, educational and general wellbeing content. Our goal is to create material that is clear, original, useful, appropriately sourced, honest about AI involvement, and responsible about finance and wellbeing boundaries.

Finance editorial standard

Finance content is created for general education and storytelling. It does not provide personalised financial, tax, legal or investment advice. Claims should not promise specific financial results.

Health and Wellness editorial standard

Health and Wellness content focuses on reflection, intentional living and general wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent medical or psychological conditions. Medical-sounding and psychological claims are not published without appropriate evidence and review.

Corrections

Where a meaningful factual error is identified, TheWhiskrMind reviews and corrects the content where practical.
